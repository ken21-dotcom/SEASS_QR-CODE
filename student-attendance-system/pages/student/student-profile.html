Privacy Policy
Data We Collect

ATTENDLY collects the following personal data to provide attendance tracking services:

  • Identity: Name, student ID number, email, contact number, program, year level, block.
  • Attendance Records: Check-in/check-out timestamps, verification method (QR, manual), event details.
  • Account Activity: Login timestamps and password changes.
How We Use Your Data
  • Record and verify attendance at campus events.
  • Generate attendance reports for administrators and SSC officers.
  • Send event announcements, reminders, and sanction notifications.
  • Manage sanctions and disciplinary records.
Data Retention

Attendance records and account data are retained for the duration of your enrollment plus the period required by institutional policy. You may request a copy of your data or request corrections using the "Request Personal Info Correction" feature.

Your Rights
  • Access your personal data and attendance history.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request account deactivation.
Request Personal Info Correction

Use this form to request a correction to your personal information (name, student ID, program, etc.). This will be sent to the registrar's office for review.

Deactivate Account
Warning: This action cannot be undone through the application.

Deactivating your account will:

  • Immediately sign you out.
  • Prevent you from signing in again.
  • Hide your profile from attendance check-ins and reports.
  • Retain your data per institutional retention policy.

To reactivate your account, you must contact the registrar or an administrator.
